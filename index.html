Pop & Learn Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Pop & Learn ("the app") is a children's educational balloon-popping game developed by Henry Bush.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no sign-up, and no login.

The app does not use advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party services that collect data. It works entirely on your device and does not need an internet connection to play.

Children's privacy

Pop & Learn is designed for children aged 2 to 6. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, including children, there is no data to share, sell, or disclose.

Purchases and links

The app contains no in-app purchases and no links that take children out of the app.

Permissions

The app does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or location.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, or need support with the app, contact: popandlearnapp@gmail.com
